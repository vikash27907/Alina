# Grievance Redressal Policy

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

This policy sets out how to raise a complaint about anything on **funwithu.in** — content, another user, a moderation decision, a payment, or how your personal data is handled — and the timelines we are bound to. It implements Rule 3(2) of the **IT Rules, 2021**, Rule 4(4)–(5) of the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**, and Section 13 of the **DPDP Act, 2023**.

## 2. How to complain

Use whichever channel suits you:

1. **In-app:** the ⚠ Report button during a call (fastest for live incidents) or the [Support](/support) page (creates a tracked ticket).
2. **Email:** grievance@funwithu.in — include your registered email, the account/content concerned, what happened, when, and any evidence.
3. **Post:** the address above, marked "Grievance".

Anyone may complain — users, non-users affected by content, parents/guardians, or authorised representatives.

## 3. Our commitments and timelines

| Complaint type | Acknowledgement | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| General grievances (IT Rules, 2021) | within **24 hours** | within **15 days** |
| Content showing the complainant in a sexual act / undress / morphed imagery, or impersonation (Rule 3(2)(b)) | within 24 hours | content removed/disabled within **24 hours** |
| Consumer/payment complaints (E-Commerce Rules, 2020) | within **48 hours** | within **1 month** |
| Data-protection requests (DPDP Act) | within 72 hours | within statutory timelines |
| Underage-user reports | immediate priority | account suspended on receipt, then verified |

"Resolution" means we tell you what we found and what we did (or why we took no action), subject to the privacy of other users.

## 4. Appeals against our decisions

If you disagree with a moderation decision (content removal, warning, suspension, ban, payout freeze) or a grievance outcome:

1. Reply on the same Support ticket or email grievance@funwithu.in with "**Appeal**" and your grounds within 30 days of the decision.
2. The appeal is reviewed by a person other than the original decision-maker where practicable.
3. You receive a reasoned outcome within **15 days**.

Child-safety terminations are not eligible for reinstatement, though factual identity errors (wrong account) will always be corrected.

## 5. If you are still unsatisfied

Depending on the subject, you may escalate to: the **Grievance Appellate Committee** constituted under Rule 3A of the IT Rules, 2021 (within 30 days of our decision, at [gac.gov.in](https://gac.gov.in)); the **Data Protection Board of India** for personal-data matters; the consumer fora under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (District/State/National Commissions, or the e-Daakhil portal); or any other forum available under law. Using our grievance process does not waive any of your legal rights.

## 6. Record keeping

All grievances, actions and appeal outcomes are logged and retained for 24 months (see the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)) so regulators can audit our compliance and you can reference prior tickets.
