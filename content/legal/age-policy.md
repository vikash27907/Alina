# Age Policy (18+)

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

funwithu.in is an **adults-only platform**. No person under **18 years of age** (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, if higher) may access or use it, in any role, for any purpose.

## 1. Why 18+

The Platform hosts live, unmoderated-in-real-time conversations between adults, flirtatious themes and paid interactions. Indian law — including the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (capacity to contract), the DPDP Act, 2023 (which defines every person under 18 as a child) and the IT Rules, 2021 — as well as our own safety standards, require us to exclude minors entirely.

## 2. How we enforce it

**All users (Customers):**

- A mandatory date-of-birth/majority declaration at signup; the account is refused without it.
- Payment instruments used for purchases are adult-issued by their nature and provide a secondary signal.
- Behavioural and report-based monitoring: any indication that an account holder may be a minor leads to immediate suspension pending verification.
- Where required by the law of a user's jurisdiction, or where doubt exists, we may require **document-based age verification** before restoring access.

**Creators:** verification is mandatory and stricter — every Creator must submit a government-issued photo ID and a matching self-photograph **before** ever appearing on camera, reviewed by our team. No unverified person may broadcast. Verification records are retained per the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 3. If we find a minor

- The account is terminated immediately and the device/network identifiers are blocked from re-registration.
- Personal data collected is deleted, subject only to records we must keep to document the incident and comply with law.
- Payments made from the account are refunded to the payment instrument's holder upon verified request.
- Any content involving a minor is preserved as evidence and **reported to the authorities** as required by the POCSO Act, 2012 and Section 67B of the IT Act, 2000. There are no exceptions.

## 4. Reporting an underage user

If you believe any user is under 18, report it immediately via the in-call ⚠ Report button or [Support](/support), or email **grievance@funwithu.in** with the account name and any details. Such reports are prioritised ahead of all other queues and handled confidentially.

## 5. Parents and guardians

If you believe your child has created an account, contact **grievance@funwithu.in**. We will suspend the account immediately on receipt, verify, delete data and process any refund as in Section 3.
