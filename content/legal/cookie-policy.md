# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on **funwithu.in**. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. What cookies we use

We deliberately keep this minimal. The Platform currently sets:

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fwu_token` | Strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in (authentication session). HttpOnly and secure; not readable by scripts. | 30 days |

**We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or cross-site analytics trackers.** If we introduce optional analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and request your consent before setting them, as required by the DPDP Act, 2023 and (for EEA/UK visitors) the ePrivacy rules.

## 2. Similar technologies

- **Local/session storage** may hold non-identifying interface preferences (e.g. UI state) on your device only.
- **WebRTC**: live video calls establish a direct media connection which necessarily uses your IP address to function. This is intrinsic to video calling, not tracking. A TURN relay, where used, is operated for connectivity, not profiling.

## 3. Your choices

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off without breaking login. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings; if you block `fwu_token` you will not be able to stay signed in. Instructions: check your browser's help pages for "manage cookies".

## 4. Changes

Material changes to our use of cookies will be announced in-app or by email and reflected in the table above with a new "Last updated" date.
