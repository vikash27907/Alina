# Community Guidelines

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

FunWithU is a live, adults-only social space. These Guidelines apply to **everyone** — Customers and Creators — in video, voice, text chat, profile content and support conversations. They exist to keep the Platform legal, safe and enjoyable. Breaking them has consequences (Section 4).

## 1. The golden rules

**Be an adult, with adults.** Every participant must be 18 or older. Any attempt to involve a minor in any way — appearing on camera, being discussed sexually, being shown in an image — is reported to law enforcement without exception, in line with Indian law including the POCSO Act, 2012 and the IT Act, 2000.

**No explicit sexual content.** FunWithU is flirtatious social entertainment, not an adult-content service. The following are prohibited for all users, on camera and in chat: nudity or exposure of intimate body parts; sexual acts, real or simulated; masturbation; sexually explicit language directed at another person after they decline; requesting or offering any of the above; and directing users to explicit content elsewhere. This rule reflects Sections 67 and 67A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, under which publishing or transmitting obscene or sexually explicit material electronically is a criminal offence in India.

**No sexual services.** Offering, requesting or arranging sexual services, escorting or prostitution — on or off the Platform — is prohibited and will be reported where required by law.

**Consent and respect.** Flirtation must be mutual. "No" ends the topic. Harassment, stalking, threats, humiliation, hate speech (attacks based on religion, caste, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation or disability), and doxxing are prohibited.

## 2. Also prohibited

- **Recording or redistribution.** Recording, screenshotting, streaming or publishing any part of a call or chat, or another user's image, without consent. This may also constitute an offence under Section 66E of the IT Act (violation of privacy).
- **Off-platform steering.** Sharing or requesting phone numbers, messaging IDs, social handles or payment details; soliciting payment outside the Platform (this also breaches the Creator Agreement).
- **Fraud & deception.** Impersonation, pretending to be a Creator/staff member, romance scams, requesting money or gifts by deception, chargeback abuse, use of bots, fake accounts or stolen payment instruments.
- **Illegal activity.** Promoting or facilitating violence, terrorism, weapons or drug sales, gambling services, money laundering, or any content unlawful under Indian law including the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
- **Self-harm content.** Encouraging or glorifying self-harm or suicide. If you tell us you are in danger we may share resources and, where legally required, notify authorities.
- **Intoxication and endangerment.** Participating while visibly severely intoxicated, driving during calls, or otherwise endangering yourself or others on camera.
- **Spam & commercial misuse.** Advertising, recruiting for other platforms, pyramid schemes, or repetitive unsolicited content.

## 3. Extra rules for Creators

Creators must additionally: be the verified account holder on camera at all times (no substitutes, no pre-recorded video); keep interactions within these Guidelines even if a Customer requests otherwise; never ask Customers for off-platform payment, gifts or contact; and never share another user's information. Full obligations are in the [Creator Agreement](/legal/creator-agreement).

## 4. Enforcement ladder

We apply the least severe action that protects users, escalating as needed:

1. **Warning** — recorded on the account.
2. **Feature restriction** — e.g. temporary loss of chat or matching.
3. **Suspension** — account locked for a stated period (typically 7–30 days).
4. **Permanent ban** — for severe or repeated violations.
5. **Payout freeze** (Creators) — earnings withheld during investigation of fraud or serious breach, released or forfeited according to the Creator Agreement.
6. **Referral to law enforcement** — mandatory for child-safety offences and other serious crimes; at our discretion (or as legally required) for threats, fraud and privacy offences.

Zero-tolerance (immediate permanent ban + report where applicable): any child-safety violation; sexual services; credible threats of violence; payment fraud.

Sanctions may be applied to all accounts we reasonably believe belong to the same person. You can contest any enforcement action through the [Grievance Redressal Policy](/legal/grievance); child-safety decisions are not subject to reinstatement.

## 5. Reporting

Use the **⚠ Report** button during any call, the **🚫 Block** button to stop being matched with someone, or open a [Support](/support) ticket. Reports are confidential. Under the IT Rules, 2021, complaints about content depicting a person in a sexual act or impersonation are prioritised and actioned within **24 hours** of receipt. We do not penalise good-faith reports, even if unfounded; knowingly false reports may themselves be sanctioned.
