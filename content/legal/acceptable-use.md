# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and is published pursuant to Rule 3(1) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021. It defines what you may not do on or to **funwithu.in**. The [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) govern behaviour between people; this AUP additionally covers legal and technical misuse of the Platform itself.

## 1. Content you may not host, display, upload, publish or transmit

In line with Rule 3(1)(b) of the IT Rules, 2021, you must not create, share or transmit content that:

1. belongs to another person and to which you have no right;
2. is obscene, pornographic, paedophilic, invasive of another's privacy (including bodily privacy), insulting or harassing on the basis of gender, or racially or ethnically objectionable;
3. is harmful to children in any way;
4. relates to or encourages money laundering, gambling, or is otherwise unlawful;
5. infringes any patent, trademark, copyright or other proprietary right;
6. deceives or misleads about the origin of a message, or knowingly communicates misinformation or patently false information;
7. impersonates another person or entity;
8. threatens the unity, integrity, defence, security or sovereignty of India, friendly relations with foreign states, or public order, or incites any cognisable offence;
9. contains a software virus or any code designed to interrupt, destroy or limit the functionality of any computer resource;
10. violates any law in force in India.

## 2. Platform integrity — you must not

- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Platform, or breach any security or authentication measure, except through a responsible-disclosure report to security@funwithu.in;
- access accounts, data or systems you are not authorised to access, or exceed your authorised access;
- use bots, scripts, scrapers or other automation to access the Platform, create accounts, or simulate activity;
- interfere with service operation, including denial-of-service attacks, flooding, or resource abuse;
- reverse engineer, decompile or copy the Platform's software except as permitted by law;
- circumvent age verification, identity verification, blocks, suspensions, bans or geographic restrictions, including by creating new accounts after a ban;
- resell, sublicense or commercially exploit the Platform, Coins or any content without our written agreement;
- misuse virtual items: buy or sell Coins or accounts outside the Platform, use stolen payment instruments, launder funds through Coin purchases or gifts, or abuse promotional credits.

## 3. Communications misuse

You must not use the Platform to send spam, chain letters or unsolicited advertising; to phish or harvest personal data of other users; or to distribute links to malware, explicit content or illegal services.

## 4. Consequences

Violations are handled under the enforcement ladder in the [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines) and may result in content removal, account suspension or termination, forfeiture of Coins or withholding of Creator earnings connected to fraud, and reporting to authorities. We may also pursue civil remedies. Under Rule 3(1)(c) of the IT Rules, 2021, we may terminate access of non-compliant users immediately.

## 5. Reporting violations

Report suspected violations via the in-call ⚠ Report button, the [Support](/support) page, or grievance@funwithu.in. Security vulnerabilities: security@funwithu.in (we ask for reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure and will not pursue good-faith researchers who respect user privacy and data integrity).
