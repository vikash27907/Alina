# Content Removal & Copyright Policy

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

This policy explains how to get content removed from funwithu.in — whether it violates your privacy or dignity, infringes your copyright or trademark, or breaks the law — and what we do when we receive such requests. It implements our obligations as an intermediary under **Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000** and the **IT Rules, 2021**, and describes our voluntary process for copyright complaints consistent with international (DMCA-style) practice.

## 1. Non-consensual or sexual imagery — priority removal

If content on the Platform shows you (or a person you legally represent) in a sexual act, in a state of undress, or is a morphed/deepfake image of you, or impersonates you:

- Report it via the in-call ⚠ Report button, the [Support](/support) page, or **grievance@funwithu.in**.
- Under **Rule 3(2)(b) of the IT Rules, 2021**, we remove or disable access to such content within **24 hours** of receiving a complaint from the individual or someone on their behalf.
- We preserve associated records for investigation and, where the content may constitute an offence (e.g. under Sections 66E/67/67A of the IT Act or the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023), we report and cooperate with law enforcement.

**Child sexual abuse material is reported to authorities immediately in every case** (IT Act Section 67B; POCSO Act, 2012), with full preservation of evidence.

## 2. Copyright and trademark complaints

FunWithU hosts live interactions and limited user content (profile photos, chat). If you believe content on the Platform infringes your copyright or trademark, send a notice to **legal@funwithu.in** containing:

1. identification of the protected work (registration details or sufficient description);
2. the exact location/URL or account and content concerned;
3. your name, address, email and relationship to the rights holder;
4. a good-faith statement that the use is not authorised by the rights owner, its agent or the law;
5. a statement, under penalty of perjury/false statement, that the notice is accurate and you are authorised to act; and
6. your physical or electronic signature.

On receiving a complete notice we will remove or disable access to the identified content **within 36 hours** and notify the uploader, who may submit a counter-notice with grounds and supporting evidence. If a valid counter-notice is received and the complainant does not initiate legal proceedings within 21 days, the content may be restored. Repeat infringers' accounts are terminated.

## 3. Government and court orders

We remove or disable access to content within **36 hours** of receiving actual knowledge in the form of a court order or notification from the appropriate government agency that content is unlawful (Rule 3(1)(d), IT Rules, 2021), and comply with lawful information requests under Rule 3(1)(j) within the prescribed timelines. We disclose user information only under valid legal process, as described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 4. Defamation, privacy and other unlawful content

For complaints that content is defamatory, invades privacy, or is otherwise unlawful but does not fall under Sections 1–3, write to grievance@funwithu.in with the specifics and any supporting court order or legal basis. Where the illegality is apparent on the face of it we act directly under the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use); where it requires adjudication we may require a court order, consistent with the Supreme Court's ruling in *Shreya Singhal v. Union of India* (2015).

## 5. Our own moderation

Independent of complaints, we proactively remove content and sanction accounts that violate the [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines). Users may contest removals via the [Grievance Redressal Policy](/legal/grievance).

## 6. Timelines summary

| Complaint type | Action time |
|---|---|
| Sexual/impersonation imagery of the complainant (Rule 3(2)(b)) | 24 hours |
| Court/government order (Rule 3(1)(d)) | 36 hours |
| Copyright/trademark notice | 36 hours from complete notice |
| Other grievances | acknowledged 24 hours; resolved within 15 days |

**Contact:** legal@funwithu.in (IP) · grievance@funwithu.in (all other removal requests)
