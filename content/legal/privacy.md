## 1. What we collect

**Account data (all users):** name or display name, email address, hashed password, account role, language preference.

**Age & identity verification data (Creators):** date of birth, country, government-issued photo ID and a self-photograph holding that ID. This is collected because verifying that every Creator is an adult is a legal and safety requirement of operating the Platform.

**Financial data:** Coin purchase history, transaction amounts and payment status (Customers); earnings, payout requests and the payout details you provide such as UPI ID or bank account (Creators). Card numbers are processed by our payment providers and are not stored by us.

**Usage & communication data:** call start/end times, duration, matching events, coin spends and gifts; support tickets, reports and appeals. **We do not record the video or audio of calls, and text chat messages are relayed live and are not stored on our servers after the call ends.** What we retain about a call is its metadata (who, when, how long, coins spent) and the text of any report or support ticket you submit.

**Technical data:** IP address, device/browser type, log and security events (e.g. login attempts), cookies as described in the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).

We do not knowingly collect data from any person under 18. If we learn we hold such data we will delete it and terminate the account.

## 3. Chat and calls — what is and is not kept

Video, audio and in-call text messages are transmitted in real time and **not stored** once the call ends. We keep call **metadata** (participants, start/end time, duration, coins spent, end reason) for billing, safety and dispute purposes. If you submit a report, the reason and details you provide are stored with the report. If we ever introduce retention of in-call messages for safety purposes, we will update this Policy and notify you before doing so.

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Payment processors and payout providers** — to process purchases and Creator payouts.
- **Infrastructure providers** (hosting, storage, email delivery) — bound by contract to process data only on our instructions.
- **KYC/verification vendors** (if/when engaged) — to verify Creator identity and age.
- **Law enforcement, courts and regulators** — where required by applicable law or valid legal order, including under the IT Act, 2000, the DPDP Act, 2023 and the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.
- **Successors** — in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, with notice to you.

We never share Creator verification documents with other users. Admin access to them is restricted and logged.

## 5. International transfers

Data is primarily stored in India. Where data is transferred outside India, we do so in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act (transfers are permitted except to countries restricted by the Central Government) and, for EEA/UK users, subject to appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

| Data | Retention |

When retention ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 7. Your rights

**Under the DPDP Act, 2023 (India)** you have the right to: access a summary of your personal data and processing activities; correction and completion of inaccurate data; **erasure** of data no longer necessary (subject to legal retention duties); grievance redressal; and to **nominate** another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity. You may withdraw consent at any time, after which we will stop the related processing unless another lawful ground applies; withdrawal may mean parts of the service cannot be provided.

To exercise any right, email **privacy@funwithu.in** or use the in-app Support page. We respond within the timelines prescribed by law. If unsatisfied, you may complain to our Grievance Officer (below) and thereafter to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

**EEA/UK users (GDPR/UK GDPR):** you additionally have rights of portability, restriction and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Our lawful bases are listed in Section 2.

**California users (CCPA/CPRA):** we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CPRA; you have rights of access, deletion and correction, exercisable as above, without discrimination.

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/WSS), hashed passwords, role-based access controls, rate limiting, audit logging of administrative actions, and restricted, logged access to verification documents. No system is perfectly secure; in the event of a personal data breach we will notify the **Data Protection Board of India and affected users** as required by Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act and other applicable law.
