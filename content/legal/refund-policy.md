# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last updated: 11 July 2026**

This policy forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and is designed to comply with the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** and the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**. It applies to purchases of Coins and any subscription packages on funwithu.in.

## 1. The general rule

Coins are a digital service consumed on use. **Purchases are final and non-refundable once Coins have been spent**, and partially used Coin packages are not refundable for the used portion. Virtual gifts and completed call minutes are final at the moment they are delivered, because the service (a live human interaction) has already been performed.

## 2. When you ARE entitled to a refund

We will refund, to your original payment method, in full or proportionally, in the following cases:

1. **Technical failure on our side.** A paid call fails, drops, or is materially unusable due to a fault of the Platform (not your device or network) — the Coins consumed by the affected minutes are re-credited automatically or on request; if you prefer, the corresponding money for those Coins is refunded.
2. **Duplicate or erroneous charges.** The same purchase charged more than once, or an amount different from the displayed price — the excess is refunded.
3. **Unauthorised / fraudulent transactions.** Purchases made without the cardholder's authorisation, reported to us — refunded following verification, and we cooperate with your bank's process.
4. **Service not delivered as described.** Coins purchased but not credited to your balance, or a paid feature that did not function — refunded or re-credited at your choice.
5. **Unused packages.** A Coin package that is **entirely unused** may be refunded on request within **7 days** of purchase.
6. **Material adverse policy change.** If we materially change the service or these terms to your detriment and you close your account within 30 days of the change, we refund the money value of your remaining unused Coins from purchases made in the previous 90 days.
7. **Statutory rights.** Any other circumstance where applicable consumer-protection law grants you a refund right that cannot be excluded. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

## 3. When refunds are NOT available

- Change of mind after Coins are spent, or dissatisfaction with a lawful, rule-compliant conversation (the service is live human time, which cannot be returned).
- Coins spent by someone you allowed to access your account.
- Accounts terminated for material breach of the Terms (e.g. fraud, child-safety violations); any remaining Coin balance in such accounts is forfeited to the extent permitted by law.
- Purchases made to circumvent a suspension or ban.

## 4. How to request a refund

Open a ticket on the [Support](/support) page or email **support@funwithu.in** with the transaction reference, date and reason. We acknowledge within **24 hours** and decide within **7 days**; approved refunds are initiated within **7 business days** of approval (bank processing times may add a few days). If you disagree with a decision, escalate under the [Grievance Redressal Policy](/legal/grievance).

## 5. Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank without first contacting us, we may suspend the account pending resolution to prevent fraud. We respond to all bank disputes with transaction evidence. Fraudulent chargeback abuse (disputing charges for services genuinely received) is a breach of the Terms and may result in termination and recovery of amounts owed.

## 6. Subscriptions and renewals

If subscription plans are offered: you may cancel auto-renewal at any time before the renewal date, effective at the end of the current period; renewal charges are refundable in full if requested within **48 hours of the renewal** provided no included minutes/Coins from the renewal have been used. Renewal reminders are sent to your registered email before each charge.

## 7. Creator payouts

This policy covers Customer purchases. Creator earnings and payouts are governed by the [Creator Agreement](/legal/creator-agreement).
